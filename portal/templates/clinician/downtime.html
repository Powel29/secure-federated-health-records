Do not delay emergency assessment or treatment while waiting for the federation. Notify the local clinical lead and use your institution's approved emergency and downtime procedures.

  1. Confirm identity using local identifiers, or assign a temporary wristband identifier for an unidentified arrival.
  2. Use local available records and direct handover. Mark unavailable history and allergies as unknown.
  3. Contact the records custodian through an approved verified channel if additional history is needed. Record the source and time in the local downtime chart.
  4. Keep clinical notes in the approved local downtime system. This portal queue stores identifiers and the procedure used only.
  5. After recovery, oversight reconciles these events into the audit chain. Clinicians reauthorize any electronic access and transcribe local notes with their original clinical time and provenance.

The encrypted federation cannot release records without its audit service. If this portal is also down, use the local paper downtime log and enter the event after recovery.
