# F9 — Limitations: PROJECT.md §5 and §6, with code/ADR corroboration

Added by X6 (fixer, round 1). Facts 1–13 are the thirteen honesty caveats of
`PROJECT.md` §5 (lines 330-427), in its order. Each cites the PROJECT.md lines
and, where one exists, the code, test or ADR that corroborates it (sources
re-used from facts-F1…F7 and facts-live). Facts 14–17 are the §6 out-of-scope
items and the D4 cut used in Section X. Where §5 is stale against the code,
the code-backed wording is given and the gap is listed under Contradictions.

1. Revocation is forward-only: rotation denies future reads but cannot retract plaintext a user already decrypted and kept. Only the patient can revoke in this build; home-authority revocation is not wired. — `PROJECT.md:335-338`; `src/lifecycle/expiry.py:86-91` (facts-F2#87); `src/api/hospital.py:1043-1124` (facts-F4#41); `docs/adr/0035-time-boxed-emergency-access-and-auto-expiry.md` (Status: Accepted); `docs/adr/0084-revocation-is-patient-only-in-this-build.md` (Status: Proposed)
2. Emergency expiry is server-enforced (a trusted timer process standing in for an HSM), not cryptographic: a compromised timer could decline to expire a key. This is reduced to tamper evidence because expiry is a forced rotation between timer-signed, audited transitions. — `PROJECT.md:339-342`; `docs/threat-model.md:43-47` (facts-F5#6); `src/lifecycle/expiry.py:24-30, 238, 646-684` (facts-F1#33, facts-F2#84); `docs/adr/0003-simulated-hsm-timer.md` (Status: Accepted)
3. No single shareholder, including the audit service, can open a record alone; this is claimed and tested, not assumed. It rests on share placement: the gate checks only that at least three distinct x are present, not which holder supplied them. — `PROJECT.md:343-344`; live claims run C3 holds, 10 system tests (facts-live#3); `src/breakglass/gate.py:235-247` (facts-F2#37); `src/breakglass/emergency_key.py:11-16` (facts-F2#36); `docs/adr/0089-weighted-3-of-4-breakglass.md` (Status: Proposed)
4. The access structure is not full CP-ABE: Shamir sharing over an AND/OR policy tree with symmetric attribute keys is not collusion-resistant as pairing-based CP-ABE is (two users could pool attribute keys to satisfy a policy neither satisfies alone), and it has no proof. — `PROJECT.md:345-351`; `src/contracts/enums.py:66-71` (facts-F3#32); `src/policy/engine.py:42-49` (facts-F3#36); `ARCHITECTURE.md:162-166` and `docs/adr/0002-secret-sharing-access-structure.md:29-33` (Status: Accepted) (facts-F3#41)
5. One shared Key Custodian process is every persona's key agent: it holds each persona's signing key, PRE key and credentials, plus share 1 of every record, in memory, and signs for them after portal login; in reality each agent would be the person's own device or enclave. The threshold is not weakened: the custodian holds share 1 only and the audit service still commits before releasing shares 3 and 4. — `PROJECT.md:352-358`; `src/api/custodian_service.py:180-192` (facts-F4#14); `src/api/custodian_service.py:1448-1580`, `src/api/audit_service.py:379-440` (facts-F4#12); `docs/adr/0043-key-custodian-and-portal-integration.md` (Status: Accepted)
6. Expiry rotation is finished after a crash or restart, not before: sessions are stored without their key, so a facility that restarts mid-window or crashes mid-rotation closes them at startup with a fresh full rotation; until then the record stays closed. A patient's revocation runs the same rotation; if a share holder is unreachable, the revocation holds but break-glass on that record stays closed until re-keyed. — `PROJECT.md:359-371`; `src/lifecycle/rotation.py:59-60, 220-291` (facts-F1#40); `src/lifecycle/expiry.py:178-187, 429-500` (facts-F1#66, facts-F1#68); `src/api/hospital.py:1061-1063, 1111-1116` (facts-F1#37); `docs/adr/0069-full-rotation-at-expiry.md`, `docs/adr/0080-revocation-without-keys-on-the-wire.md`, `docs/adr/0081-restart-and-crash-recovery.md` (all Status: Proposed)
7. Unidentified patients get no history: break-glass is bound to a `patient_id` chosen from a logged directory lookup by name (date of birth optional; ambiguous matches shown, never auto-selected; a lookup that cannot be audited is not shown). A patient with no identification gets only a temporary encounter identifier (`TEMP-…`), and the encounter is linked to a patient only after a logged selection plus a written identity confirmation of at least 20 characters. The scenario therefore assumes identification. — `PROJECT.md:372-380`; `portal/clinician/views.py:8-11, 219-246`; `portal/clinician/encounters.py:117-129, 140-148`; `src/api/hospital.py:1401-1411` (facts-F2#12); `docs/adr/0033-emergency-patient-identification.md` (Status: Accepted); `docs/adr/0059-demo-patient-carries-id.md` (Status: Proposed); `docs/adr/0060-clinical-workflow-remediation.md` (Status: "Implemented locally; contract changes require team review before merge")
8. The audit service is a single point of availability: every qualifying 3-share set contains an audit share, and the home authority's share 2 covers only a lost share 1 (kept in custodian memory). It does not help when the custodian (also the clinician's key agent) or the audit service is down; an audit restart loses shares 3 and 4 of every record until re-keyed; and, from the code (not observed in a run), a restarted audit service gets a fresh signing key and would refuse to start on a non-empty chain. — `PROJECT.md:381-392`; `src/breakglass/emergency_key.py:11-16` (facts-F2#36); `src/api/custodian_service.py:222, 1448-1455` (facts-F2#62); `src/audit/share_registry.py:13-16` (facts-F2#63); `deploy/asgi/audit.py:43-49`, `src/api/audit_service.py:201-213` (facts-F2#64); `docs/adr/0089-weighted-3-of-4-breakglass.md` (Status: Proposed)
9. Revoking a credential does not revoke a released key: one attribute key per (authority, attribute value) is shared by all holders and never rotated, and record rotation re-seals under the same keys, so a revoked holder who kept a key still satisfies its leaves. — `PROJECT.md:393-399`; `src/authority/issuance.py:151-153` (facts-F3#37); `docs/adr/0075-credential-revocation-does-not-revoke-released-keys.md` (Status: Proposed)
10. The PRE is bidirectional and the gate decrypts: `rk = sk_a·sk_b^-1` inverts to the reverse key, needs both secret keys and has no proof; break-glass uses no re-encryption — the facility unwraps the DEK and delivers it to the clinician by a KEM. — `PROJECT.md:400-405`; `src/pre/elgamal.py:64-93` (facts-F3#43); grep over `src/` (facts-F3#44); `src/breakglass/gate.py:250-268` (facts-F3#46); `docs/adr/0076-pre-is-bidirectional.md` (Status: Proposed) (facts-F3#50)
11. "No plaintext on the wire" holds under TLS only. On the plain profile §5 lists record plaintext, DEKs and attribute keys as crossing, because the facility encrypts and decrypts server-side. The p55 plain capture (positive control) found record plaintext, four attribute keys and, at ingest, the emergency key, but no DEK and no share. TLS certificates are self-signed and server-only, with no TLS version or cipher pinned. — `PROJECT.md:406-411`; `bench/results/p55_wire_checks.json` `[1].evidence.plain_hits` (facts-F6#6, facts-F6#37); `wire/tls.py:22-80` (facts-F4#34); `docs/adr/0073-claim-1-scoped-to-tls.md` (Status: Proposed)
12. The audit chain is pseudonymous, not anonymous: it names patients by store `patient_id` (no name or date of birth), so a chain reader learns which pseudonymous patient was accessed, by whom and when. — `PROJECT.md:412-418`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:536-542` (facts-F4#42); `docs/adr/0085-no-name-or-dob-in-the-chain-patient-id-is-the-pseudonym.md:45-64` (Status: Proposed)

The audit service is trusted: stored-entry tampering is detected (C5) and a facility refuses a truncated chain, but a compromised audit service could release shares 3 and 4 without a durable entry or show different chains to different parties, as there are no proofs of presence or extension; "no log entry, no key" holds against everyone but the audit service. — `PROJECT.md:419-427` (facts-F5#9); shares 3 and 4 per `src/breakglass/emergency_key.py:11-16` and `src/api/audit_service.py:379-440` (facts-F2#36, facts-F4#12); `docs/adr/0088-novelty-restated-as-combination.md` (Status: Proposed); `docs/adr/0034-audit-hash-chain-and-share-release.md` (Status: Accepted); `docs/adr/0083-audit-high-water-mark-on-facility-disk.md` (Status: Proposed)
