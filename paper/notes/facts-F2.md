## A. The Emergency Assertion

1. `EmergencyAssertion` has ten fields: `assertion_id: UUID` (one-time), `patient_id: Identifier`, `clinician_id: Identifier`, `facility_id: Identifier`, `reason_code: ReasonCode` (constrained enum, never free text), `issued_at: AwareDatetime`, `expires_at: AwareDatetime`, `nonce: B64Bytes`, `clinical_context_digest: str = ""`, `signature: B64Bytes` (ECDSA by the clinician's certificate key). — src/contracts/messaging.py:39-48
2. Structural validators on the contract: `expires_at` must be after `issued_at`, the nonce must be at least `MIN_ANTIREPLAY_NONCE_BYTES` = 16 bytes (128 bits), and the signature must be non-empty; a failure raises `ValueError` before the protocol runs. — src/contracts/messaging.py:50-73; src/contracts/base.py:61
3. `create_emergency_assertion` draws `assertion_id` with `uuid4()` and a 16-byte nonce with `secrets.token_bytes`, defaults `issued_at` to now (UTC) and the lifetime to 15 minutes, and signs with `crypto_core.sign` (ECDSA P-256 key of the clinician). — src/breakglass/assertion.py:143-162; src/breakglass/assertion.py:65
4. The signature covers every field except the signature itself, encoded with `crypto_core.encode_fields` under the domain tag `"ehr/emergency-assertion/v1"` (length-prefixed fields, ADR-0077); timestamps are encoded as UTC ISO-8601. — src/breakglass/assertion.py:54, src/breakglass/assertion.py:73-103
5. Who signs: the clinician's private key, held inside the custodian (the clinician's key agent), which builds the assertion in `prepare_unlock` → `create_emergency_assertion`; the key does not leave the custodian. — src/api/custodian_service.py:1483-1497; src/custodian/__init__.py:77-94; docs/adr/0068-clinician-signed-breakglass-transition.md (Status: Proposed)
6. Record binding: `clinical_context_digest = SHA-256(JSON[record_id, justification.strip(), sensitive.strip()])` (hex), carried inside the signed payload; `validate_clinical_context` raises `ValueError` if the digest does not match the record id and justification texts presented. — src/breakglass/assertion.py:212-229
7. `validate_emergency_assertion` runs, in this order, and fails closed at the first failure: (1) signature against the certificate's public key → `InvalidSignatureError`; (2) certificate chain leaf → facility CA → trusted root at time `now` → `ChainValidationError` / `CertificateExpiredError` (a bad link signature is re-raised as `ChainValidationError`); (3) `clinician_id` equals the certificate subject CN → `ClinicianMismatchError`; (3a) certificate serial not in `revoked_serials` → `CertificateRevokedError`; (4) `facility_id` equals `expected_facility_id` → `FacilityMismatchError`; (5) `expires_at > now` → `AssertionExpiredError`; lifetime `expires_at - issued_at ≤ max_ttl` → `AssertionLifetimeError`; `issued_at ≤ now + clock_skew` → `AssertionLifetimeError`; (6) consume `assertion_id` → `ReplayedAssertionError`. — src/breakglass/assertion.py:300-364
8. Line-level locations of the checks in fact 7: signature 303-304; chain 309-319; CN 322-330; revocation 333-337; facility 340-344; expiry 347-351; lifetime 353-357; future-dated 358-361; replay 364. — src/breakglass/assertion.py:303-364
9. Signature runs first so a tampered assertion is reported as tampered, and consumption runs last so a forged or otherwise invalid assertion cannot burn a legitimate id (ADR-0030 §4 ordering). — src/breakglass/assertion.py:294-298, src/breakglass/assertion.py:363-364; tests/security/test_phase3_assertion_security.py::TestAssertionReplay::test_forged_assertion_does_not_burn_legitimate_id; tests/security/test_phase3_assertion_security.py::TestAssertionLifetime::test_rejected_lifetime_does_not_burn_the_assertion_id

11. Exception classes (all subclass `BreakglassError` → `CryptoError`): `AssertionExpiredError`, `AssertionLifetimeError`, `CertificateRevokedError`, `ClinicianMismatchError`, `FacilityMismatchError`, `ReplayedAssertionError`. — src/breakglass/exceptions.py:15, 24, 32, 41, 45, 53, 66
12. Patient binding is enforced at the facility, not in `validate_emergency_assertion`: after the record is loaded and before any lifecycle change, `record.patient_id != assertion.patient_id` is refused with HTTP 403 and an audited denial `breakglass_patient_binding`. — src/api/hospital.py:1401-1411; docs/adr/0044-breakglass-patient-binding-and-expiry-state.md (Status: Proposed; the state-reconciliation half is superseded by ADR-0050)

14. ## C. Replay protection

## I. How the emergency session ends

83. Expiry is driven by a background monitor thread (`start_monitor`, 0.5 s poll) started by the deployed facility, plus explicit `expire_session` / `check_and_expire_due`; no administrator action is needed. — src/lifecycle/expiry.py:740-770; deploy/asgi/facility.py:152-157; docs/adr/0035-time-boxed-emergency-access-and-auto-expiry.md (Status: Accepted)
84. `expire_session` order: claim the session; sign `EMERGENCY_OPEN → EXPIRED` (consumes its event id); run the forced rotation; sign `EXPIRED → return_state` (`GRANTED` or `REVOKED`); audit each transition before mirroring it into the store; then overwrite the session's emergency-key reference with 32 zero bytes and drop the session. A failure is retried with backoff 1 s, 2 s, 4 s … capped at 60 s. — src/lifecycle/expiry.py:543-637, src/lifecycle/expiry.py:96-97, src/lifecycle/expiry.py:800-811
85. The rotation (ADR-0069) generates a fresh emergency key and a fresh 3-of-4 split and hands share 1 to the custodian, share 2 to the owner authority, shares 3 and 4 to the audit service; each holder replaces only for a facility caller, only while the store shows the record `expired` or `revoked`, and only for a later generation. — src/lifecycle/rotation.py:265-266; src/api/rotation.py:199-210; src/api/audit_service.py:345-377; src/audit/share_registry.py:89-115; docs/adr/0069-full-rotation-at-expiry.md (Status: Proposed); docs/adr/0089-weighted-3-of-4-breakglass.md (Status: Proposed)
86. The record does not return to baseline until the rotation is stored; afterwards the old DEK, root secret, emergency key, shares and search tokens open nothing. Pre-expiry shares not restoring search is a tested claim. — src/lifecycle/expiry.py:582-588; tests/security/test_p51_system_claims.py::test_c4_pre_expiry_shares_do_not_restore_search
87. Limits: zeroization is best-effort in Python; plaintext a clinician already decrypted is not retractable; the timer is a trusted process. — src/lifecycle/expiry.py:86-91; docs/adr/0035-time-boxed-emergency-access-and-auto-expiry.md (Status: Accepted)

## Contradictions found

Code wins in every case below.

1. **PROJECT.md §5 item 13 says "share 3" alone** ("nothing stops a compromised audit service from releasing share 3 without a durable entry"). The audit service holds and releases shares 3 and 4 (src/breakglass/emergency_key.py:37; src/api/audit_service.py:419-446). — PROJECT.md:422
2. **ARCHITECTURE.md §5 trust boundaries: "trusted to hold one share".** It holds two (x = 3, 4). — ARCHITECTURE.md:338
3. **ARCHITECTURE.md §5 step 7: rotation's "new shares go to the custodian and the audit service".** Code also hands share 2 to the owner authority (src/api/rotation.py:199-210; ADR-0089 §6). — ARCHITECTURE.md:323-324
4. **ARCHITECTURE.md §8 assertion struct omits `clinical_context_digest`**, and its five-step validation list omits the separate chain check, revocation, the 15-minute lifetime cap and the 30 s future-dating check that code runs (src/contracts/messaging.py:47; src/breakglass/assertion.py:303-364). The "five-point" wording in src/breakglass/assertion.py:4-11 and src/breakglass/__init__.py:3 undercounts the same way.
5. **ADR-0032 (Accepted) says the replay check runs "if provided"** and that payloads are `\x1f`-delimited. Code makes `consumed_store` (and `revoked_serials`) required and uses `encode_fields` (src/breakglass/assertion.py:244-245, 103). A stale comment in src/breakglass/gate.py:78-80 still claims assertion.py uses the unit separator.
6. **ADR-0057 (Proposed) says joins need "the same 2-of-3 threshold, so the audit service still commits its entry before releasing share 3".** Code: 3-of-4, shares 3 and 4 (src/breakglass/emergency_key.py:29-37).
7. **ADR-0067 (Proposed) title and text say "share 3" and "break-glass is 2-of-2 between the custodian and the audit service".** Overtaken by ADR-0089, but ADR-0067's status line does not say so.
8. **ADR-0069 (Proposed) says the rotated emergency key is "split 2-of-3", that share 1 goes to the custodian and share 3 to the audit service, and that "Share 2 is still generated and discarded".** Code splits 3-of-4 and deposits all four shares with three holders (src/lifecycle/rotation.py:265-266; src/api/rotation.py:199-210).
9. **ADR-0044 (Proposed) says the custodian "releases share 3 before the facility is called"**; ADR-0065 (Proposed) context says "released share 3 again"; ADR-0079 (Proposed) describes sealing "share 3" to the gate, a `share3_sealed` canary, and a 2.27 ms COMMIT-before-release measurement from a 2026-10-15 capture made before the 3-of-4 change. ADR-0089 says the captures must be re-recorded, so that number must not be quoted as current.
10. **ADR-0035 (Accepted) describes expiry as `EXPIRED → GRANTED` and the audit callback as not yet wired.** Code returns to `GRANTED` or `REVOKED` (src/lifecycle/expiry.py:220-221) and the deployed facility wires the callback and starts the monitor (deploy/asgi/facility.py:152-157).
11. **`facility_id` meaning.** The contract comment ("where the emergency is declared", src/contracts/messaging.py:42), ARCHITECTURE.md:515 and assertion.py:127 describe the declaring facility; the custodian actually writes the record's own facility (src/api/custodian_service.py:1416-1423, 1488), as ADR-0063 notes. At share holders the facility check is tautological (src/api/share_release.py:132).
12. **Event-type name.** Docstrings name the release event `breakglass_share_release` (src/audit/share_custody.py:63; src/contracts/audit.py:39); the custodian sends `breakglass_share_released` (src/api/custodian_service.py:1543). Cosmetic, but the paper should quote the latter if it names the event.
13. **ADR numbering.** docs/adr/0082-key-release-proof-of-possession.md is headed "ADR-0075" on line 1, while the tracker lists it as 0082.
14. CONVENTIONS.md:91 uses "add 2-of-3 split" as an example commit message. It is an example, not a claim, but it is the only remaining "2-of-3" in the four core docs.
